About BSP Financial Group Limited

BSP Financial Group Limited provides commercial banking and financial services in Papua New Guinea, Fiji, Solomon Islands, Cook Islands, Tonga, Samoa, Vanuatu, Cambodia, and Laos. It offers personal banking services, such as saving accounts and term deposits; personal, education, and personal asset loans; and life, health, and mortgage insurance products. The company also provides business banking services, including business, solicitor's trust, and SME business current and deposit accounts; business overdrafts; insurance premium funding; and asset financing, bridging, tailored business, commercial property investment, residential property investment, construction development, seasonal finance, and SME business and package loans.
