About Brambles Limited

Brambles Limited provides supply chain logistics solutions. The company operates through CHEP Americas, CHEP EMEA, and CHEP Asia-Pacific segments. It offers pallets, crates, and containers to transport goods in supply chains through its share and reuse network.
