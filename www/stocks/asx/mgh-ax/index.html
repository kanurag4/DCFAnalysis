About MAAS Group Holdings Limited

MAAS Group Holdings Limited, together with subsidiaries, engages in civil, construction and hire, residential and commercial real estate, and manufacturing businesses in Australia. The company engages in the construction of civil infrastructure, roads, dams, renewables, and mining infrastructure; above and underground plant hire for major infrastructure and tunnelling projects; and provision of electrical infrastructure, modular electrical manufacturing, communications, and specialised services. It also develops, invests, builds, and sells residential land and housing properties.
