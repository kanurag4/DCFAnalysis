About Bendigo and Adelaide Bank Limited

Bendigo and Adelaide Bank Limited engages in the provision of banking and other financial services to retail customers and small to medium sized businesses in Australia. It operates through Consumer, Business and Agribusiness, and Corporate segments. The company offers transaction and savings account; term deposit; managed funds; share trading; margin lending; and private banking services.
