About Deterra Royalties Limited

Deterra Royalties Limited operates as a royalty investment company in Australia, the United States, Mexico, Zambia, Peru, Canada, and Kenya. The company operates through the Bulk, Base, Battery, Precious, and Others segments. It acquires and manages a portfolio of existing royalties and similar instruments, including iron ore, mineral sands, copper, lithium, and gold.
