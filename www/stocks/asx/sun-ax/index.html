About Suncorp Group Limited

Suncorp Group Limited provides insurance products and services to retail, corporate, and commercial customers in Australia and New Zealand. The company operates through Consumer Insurance; Commercial & Personal Injury; Suncorp New Zealand; and Corporate & Internal Reinsurance segments. It provides insurance products, including home and contents, motor, and boat insurance products; and commercial motor, commercial property, marine, industrial special risks, public liability and professional indemnity, workers' compensation, and compulsory third party products.
