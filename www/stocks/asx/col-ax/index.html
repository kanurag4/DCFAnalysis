About Coles Group Limited

Coles Group Limited operates as a grocery retailer in Australia. It operates through the Supermarkets and Liquor segments. The company offers grocery, fresh produce, meat, and non-food products through its full-service supermarkets and digital platforms; home delivery, including same-day and overnight drop, go services, or pick up through coles.com.au; and wines, spirits, and beers through Coles Liquor and Liquorland stores.
