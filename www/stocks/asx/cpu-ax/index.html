About Computershare Limited

Computershare Limited provides issuer, corporate trust, employee share plans, and other services. It offers issuer services, including register maintenance, corporate actions, stakeholder relationship management, and entity compliance and governance solutions; corporate trust services, including asset-backed securities, statutory trust, longevity, mortgage-backed securities, CLO and loan, business escrow, exchange, collateral trust, catastrophe bond, loan administration and agency, institutional custody, corporate finance, municipal debt, successor trustee, and collateral agency services; and property rental services, as well as childcare voucher administration services. The company also provides utility services; communication services for banks, credit unions, financial services providers, and commercial financing providers; transfer agent services, such as corporate actions, IPO and listing events, annual general meetings, alternative investments, capital solutions, global capital markets, and asset reunification services.
