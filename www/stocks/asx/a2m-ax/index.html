About The a2 Milk Company Limited

The a2 Milk Company Limited, together with its subsidiaries, sells A2-type protein type branded milk and related products in Australia, New Zealand, China, rest of Asia, and the United States. The company offers infant milk formula; other nutritional products and milk; manufactures and sells nutritional and ingredients products, as well as provides rent, royalty, and licensing services. It offers its products under the a2 Milk and a2 Platinum brands.
