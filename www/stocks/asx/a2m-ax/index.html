About The a2 Milk Company Limited

The a2 Milk Company Limited, together with its subsidiaries, sells A2-type protein type branded milk and related products in Australia, New Zealand, China, rest of Asia, and the United States. The company offers infant milk formula, milk, and other nutritional products under the a2 Milk and a2 Platinum brands. It also provides rent, royalty, and licensing services.
