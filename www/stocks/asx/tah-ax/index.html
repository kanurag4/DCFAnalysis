About Tabcorp Holdings Limited

Tabcorp Holdings Limited, together with its subsidiaries, engages in the provision of gambling, entertainment, and integrity services in Australia. It operates through Wagering and Media and Integrity Services segments. The Wagering and Media segment offers totalisator and fixed odds betting on racing, sports, and other events through a network of TAB agencies, hotels and clubs, and on-course operations, as well as through retail, web, a digital app on mobile devices and phone via a call centre.
