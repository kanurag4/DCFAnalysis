About Sigma Healthcare Limited

Sigma Healthcare Limited operates as a retail pharmacy franchisor and pharmaceutical wholesale and distributor to community pharmacies primarily in Australia. It offers support services to a network of franchised retail pharmacies; third and fourth party logistics services to pharmaceutical manufacturers and other supplier partners; and health services. The company operates stores under the Chemist Warehouse, Amcal, and Discount Drug stores brand names.
