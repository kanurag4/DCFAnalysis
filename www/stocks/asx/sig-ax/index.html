About Sigma Healthcare Limited

Sigma Healthcare Limited operates as a retail pharmacy franchisor and pharmaceutical wholesale and distributor to community pharmacies in Australia, and internationally. It is involved in the wholesale distribution of pharmaceutical, medical, healthcare and beauty products to a network of franchised retail pharmacies, and other third parties; advertising and marketing services; and provision of support services to a network of retail pharmacies. The company also provides MPS Connect, a end-to-end medication management solution.
