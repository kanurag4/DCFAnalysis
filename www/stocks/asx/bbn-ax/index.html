About Baby Bunting Group Limited

Baby Bunting Group Limited, together with its subsidiaries, engages in the retail of maternity and baby goods through its retail and online stores in Australia and New Zealand. The company offers car seats, cots, manchester, and associated accessories; prams and strollers; nursery furniture, bedding, changing and nappy stations, baby monitors, sleepwear, nursery storage, sleep aids, nursery sets and decor, sleep support, storage baskets and hangers, and decorative decor; gift cards and sets; pregnancy and postpartum products, maternity and nursing clothing, breastfeeding essentials, and nappy bags for mums; and bathing, skin care and lotions, teethers, dummies and soothers, thermometers, first aid, nappy changing, potty and toilet training, and laundry products, as well as hair, nail, and oral care products. It also provides babywear, toddler, and kids' clothing; baby and toddler carriers, slings, toddler harnesses, and carrier accessories; and safety products, including audio and video monitors, night lights, ear protection, scratch mittens, play pens, baby gates, step stools, locks, latches, and plugs.
