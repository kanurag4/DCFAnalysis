About SEEK Limited

SEEK Limited, together with its subsidiaries, provides online employment marketplace services in Australia, New Zealand, Southeast Asia, Hong Kong, the United Kingdom, Europe, and internationally. The company's job advertisement solutions include Lite ad, which is a free ad designed for non-urgent; Basic ad, which offers search visibility and candidate recommendations; Advanced ad, which offers increased search visibility and AI candidate targeting for specialized or competitive roles; and Premium ad for business-critical or time-sensitive roles. It also offers sourcing solutions, such as Talent Search, which allows hirers to search candidate database; SEEK Pass that helps candidates stand out by verifying credentials on their application and profile, as well as helping hirers assess candidates; Employer Brand Boost, which allows hirers to amplify their employee value proposition; SEEKGrad that assists employers with early-career and graduate recruitment; JobAdder, which serves as an applicant tracking system and customer management platform for recruiters and talent acquisition teams; Sidekicker that serves as a labour marketplace providing on-demand labour across hospitality, events, retail, customer service, aged care, warehousing, and logistics industries.
