About Harvey Norman Holdings Limited

Harvey Norman Holdings Limited engages in the integrated retail, franchise, property, and digital system businesses. The company engages in the retail of furniture, bedding, computers, and communications and consumer electrical products. It also owns, develops, and manages retail complexes, as well as acts as a lessor of premises to Harvey Norman, Domayne, and Joyce Mayne franchisees, and other third parties.
