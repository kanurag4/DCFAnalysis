About REA Group Limited

REA Group Limited, together with its subsidiaries, engages in digital advertising business in Australia, Asia, and North America. It provides property and property-related services on websites and mobile applications. The company operates residential, commercial, and share property sites, such as realestate.com.au, realcommercial.com.au, Flatmates.com.au, property.com.au, housing.com, and realtor.com.
