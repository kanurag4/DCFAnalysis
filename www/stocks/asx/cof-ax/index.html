About Centuria Office REIT

Centuria Property Funds Limited is Australia's largest ASX listed pure-play office REIT. COF owns a portfolio of high quality office assets situated in core submarkets throughout Australia. COF is overseen by a hands-on, active manager and provides investors with income and the opportunity for capital growth from a pure-play portfolio of high-quality Australian office assets.
