About JB Hi-Fi Limited

JB Hi-Fi Limited retails home consumer products. It operates through four segments: JB Hi-Fi Australia, JB Hi-Fi New Zealand, The Good Guys, and e&s. The company provides computers and tablets; IT accessories and PC parts; headphones, speakers, and audio; smart homes; mobile phones; home appliances comprising large and small appliances, and personal care devices; gaming devices; turntables and vinyl accessories; and collectibles and merchandise, such as trading cards.
