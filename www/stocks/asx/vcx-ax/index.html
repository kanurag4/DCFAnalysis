About Vicinity Centres

Vicinity Centres (Vicinity or the Group) is one of Australia's leading retail property groups. With a fully integrated asset management platform, and 25 billion dollars in retail assets under management across 49 shopping centres, making it the second largest listed manager of Australian retail property. The Group has a Direct Portfolio with interests in 48 shopping centres (including the DFO Brisbane business) and manages 25 assets on behalf of Strategic Partners.
