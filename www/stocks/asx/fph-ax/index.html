About Fisher & Paykel Healthcare Corporation Limited

Fisher & Paykel Healthcare Corporation Limited, together with its subsidiaries, designs, manufactures, markets, and sells medical device products and systems in North America, Europe, the Asia Pacific, and internationally. The company provides its products for use in acute and chronic respiratory care, and surgery, as well as the treatment of obstructive sleep apnea (OSA) in the home and hospital. It also offers adult respiratory products, including airvo system, optiflow nasal high flow therapy, invasive ventilation, and noninvasive ventilation.
