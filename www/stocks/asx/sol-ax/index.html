About WHSP Holdings Limited

WHSP Holdings Limited, an investment company, engages in investing various industries and asset classes in Australia. It operates through six segments: Listed Companies, Private Companies, Emerging Companies Portfolio, Real Assets, Credit Portfolio, and Treasury. The company invests in largely uncorrelated listed companies; managed listed equities; unlisted and growing companies; credit related financial instruments; and property development.
