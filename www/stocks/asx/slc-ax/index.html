About Superloop Limited

Superloop Limited, together with its subsidiaries, operates as a telecommunications and internet service provider in Australia. It operates through Wholesale, Business, and Consumer segments. The Wholesale segment offers NBN access, NBN enterprise ethernet, internet access & IP transit, Australian intercapital capacity, Dark Fibre, Fixed wireless access, international ethernet, wavelength and international subsea cable capacity to large scale telecommunications, and data and technology customers.
