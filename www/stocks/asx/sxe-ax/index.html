About Southern Cross Electrical Engineering Limited

Southern Cross Electrical Engineering Limited, together with its subsidiaries, provides electrical, instrumentation, communications, security, fire, and maintenance services and products in Australia. The company operates through SCEE Electrical, Heyday, Trivantage, and Force Fire segments. It installs electrical and data communications cabling products and systems; designs, manufactures, and installs commercial and industrial switchboards; and provides security system solutions, including consultation, design, supply, installation, integration, preventative maintenance, access control, workforce access management, intruder detection, perimeter solutions and gates, nurse call and duress systems, and CCTV servicing, as well as designs, installs, inspects, tests, and maintains fire protection systems.
