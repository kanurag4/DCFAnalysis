About TPG Telecom Limited

TPG Telecom Limited provides telecommunications services to consumer, business, enterprise, and government and wholesale customers in Australia. It also sells mobile handsets, mobile accessory, and fixed modem devices. In addition, the company owns and operates the Vodafone mobile network products.
