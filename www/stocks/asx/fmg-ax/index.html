About Fortescue Ltd

Fortescue Ltd engages in the exploration, development, production, processing, and sale of iron ore in Australia, China, and internationally. The company explores for copper and lithium deposits; and rare earth elements. It owns and operates rail and port facilities; power system integrating solar and wind generation, battery energy storage systems, firming capability, and high voltage transmission infrastructures.
