About COWAY Co., Ltd.

COWAY Co., Ltd. engages in the production and sale of environmental home appliances in South Korea and internationally. The company provides air purifiers, water purifiers, bidets, juicers, water softeners, electric range, dehumidifier, humidifiers, kitchen/home appliances, mattress/massage chair, and filter/consumables; and Smart Air Care service, which provides customized solution for air conditioning.
