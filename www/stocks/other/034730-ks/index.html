About SK Inc.

SK Inc., together with its subsidiaries, engages in petroleum refining, telecommunications, wholesale and retail, chemicals, construction, and other businesses in Korea. It is involved in city gas and power generation; gas stations; sale of asphalt; energy business; resource development; LNG business; electricity and other electric wire installation; new regeneration; and hydrogen business. The company also manufactures and sells chemicals and petrochemicals; lubricant oils; separators for secondary cells; lithium batteries; storage batteries; industrial gas; non-memory and other electronic integrated circuit; household appliances; video and audio equipment; electronic books; steel; semiconductor component; telecommunication equipment; automotive products and parts; biodegradable plastic materials; organic light emitting diode material; silicon wafer; pharmaceuticals; and electric vehicle chargers.
