About Hannover Rück SE

Hannover Rück SE, together with its subsidiaries, provides reinsurance products and services in Germany, the United Kingdom, France, Europe, the United States, Asia, Australia, Africa, and internationally. It operates through Property & Casualty Reinsurance; and Life & Health Reinsurance segments. The company provides property and casualty reinsurance solutions for agricultural, livestock, and bloodstock; aviation and space; natural catastrophe; credit, surety, and political risks; cyber; digital; marine and upstream energy; natural catastrophes; advanced and facultative business, insurance-linked securities and structured reinsurance; and parametric solutions.
