About COVER Corporation

COVER Corporation distribute videos and music through VTubers distribution platform in Japan. It also develops characters and anime-look avatars; plans and produces events; and offers video content, such as live streams, music videos, and animations through video distribution, as well as membership subscriptions, advertising, and promotions services. In addition, the company is involved in the electronic commerce, VTuber production, and media mix businesses, plan and develop of merchandise, licenses/tie-ins, comics, animation and other activities; and development of Metaverse Holoearth, focusing on sandbox games that allow users to act freely in a virtual space.
