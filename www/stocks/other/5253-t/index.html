About COVER Corporation

COVER Corporation engages in live streaming activities through VTubers video distribution platform in Japan, the United States, and internationally. The company offers video content, including YouTube live streaming 2D and 3D services, game and interactive streams, variety shows, music streaming and releases, and 3D animation services; and advertising services on video streaming platforms. It also hosts online and offline concerts, and various events starring VTubers.
