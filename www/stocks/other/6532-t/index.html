About Baycurrent, Inc.

Baycurrent, Inc., together with its subsidiaries, provides consulting services in Japan. The company offers various services, such as AI, transformation, sustainability GX, strategy and corporate finance, customer experience, digital technology, data analytics, technology innovation, global business, marketing and sales, m and a, supply chain and operations, intelligent automation, talent and organization, cost management, engineering and manufacturing, cloud, digital commerce, infrastructure, security, and managed services. It is also involved in the management, operations, and IT consulting and implementation business; and provision of system consulting and system integration services.
