About ORION Corp.

ORION Corp., together with its subsidiaries, manufactures and sells various confectionery products in South Korea, China, and internationally. The company offers pies, snacks, biscuits, candies, gums, and chocolates; protein drinks/powders and bars/balls bars; bar and oat products; and mineral water under the Dr.You, Market O, Market O Nature, and Jeju yongamsoo brands. The company also produces and distributes films; and provides in vitro diagnostics for early detection of cancer and vaccines for contagious diseases.
