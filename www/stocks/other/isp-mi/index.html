About Intesa Sanpaolo S.p.A.

Intesa Sanpaolo S.p.A. engages in the provision of various financial products and services in Italy, Central/Eastern Europe, the Middle East, and North Africa. It operates through six segments: Banca dei Territori, IMI Corporate & Investment Banking, International Banks, Asset Management, Private Banking, and Insurance.
