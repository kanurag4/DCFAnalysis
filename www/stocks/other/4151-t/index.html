About Kyowa Kirin Co., Ltd.

Kyowa Kirin Co., Ltd. engages in the research, development, manufacturing, and marketing of pharmaceuticals in Japan, the Americas, Europe, Asia, and internationally. The company offers Ziftomenib, an oral menin inhibitor for the treatment of adult relapsed or refractory and acute myeloid leukemia with a nucelophosmin1 mutation; and Lumicef for the treatment of palmoplantar pustulosis.
