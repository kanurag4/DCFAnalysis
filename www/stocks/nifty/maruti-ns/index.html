About Maruti Suzuki India Limited

Maruti Suzuki India Limited manufactures and sells motor vehicles, parts, and accessories in India. The company offers passenger and commercial vehicles, including hatchbacks, sedans, SUVs, MPVs, and vans, as well as light commercial vehicles. It is also involved in the facilitation of pre-owned car sales, fleet management, and car financing activities.
