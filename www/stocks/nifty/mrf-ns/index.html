About MRF Limited

MRF Limited, together with its subsidiaries, engages in the manufacture and sale of rubber products in India and internationally. The company provides tires for passenger cars, two wheelers, farms, trucks, OTR, SCV, LCV, pick up, three-wheeler, MCV, ICV, tubes, and flap products; tread rubber product; and manufactures specialty coatings. It is also involved in the paints and coats, sports goods, pretreads, and motorsports businesses; and heavy-duty truck/bus tyres, light truck, MUV/RCV/passenger cars, farm services, military services, fork-lift, off the road/earth movers, and MRF commercial tyres products.
