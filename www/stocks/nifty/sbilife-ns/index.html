About SBI Life Insurance Company Limited

SBI Life Insurance Company Limited operates as a life insurance company in India. The company's life insurance business comprises individual and group life insurance products, including participating, non-participating, pension, group gratuity, group leave encashment, group superannuation, group annuity, unit-linked and variable insurance products, health, and micro insurance. It also provides accident and disability benefits, level terms, and critical illness insurance products.
