About these pages

Each NIFTY 500 stock listed here has its own free valuation page, computed mechanically from Yahoo Finance data. Most use DCF or DDM (intrinsic value, margin of safety, bear/base/bull scenarios); genuine negative-cashflow growth companies instead use a Multi-Stage DCF and EV/Sales model that also shows what growth rate the current price already implies. Each page is a point-in-time snapshot and shows the date it was computed. Not financial advice.
