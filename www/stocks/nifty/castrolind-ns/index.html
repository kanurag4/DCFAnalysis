About Castrol India Limited

Castrol India Limited manufactures and markets automotive and industrial lubricants in India and internationally. The company offers engine and transmission oils, brake fluids, coolants, cleaners, and greases; EV transmission fluids, thermal fluids, and greases; and brake fluid chain lubricants, fork oils, gear oils, coolants, diesel exhaust fluids, hydraulic fluids, rust preventives, cleaners, and forming oils. The company offers its products under the Castor EDGE, Castrol's MAGNATEC, Castrol GTX, Castrol ON, Castrol Activ, Castrol POWER1, Castrol GO!, Castrol VECTON, Castrol CRB, and Castrol RX brands.
